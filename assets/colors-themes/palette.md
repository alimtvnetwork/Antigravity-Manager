# AG Manager brand palette

## Core colors

| Role | Name | HEX | RGB | HSL | Guidance |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Primary | Lift Blue | `#2878f0` | `rgb(40, 120, 240)` | `hsl(216, 87%, 55%)` | Main brand anchor, actions, and the upper orbit. |
| Secondary | Vector Green | `#16a97a` | `rgb(22, 169, 122)` | `hsl(161, 77%, 37%)` | Automation success, momentum, and the lower orbit. |
| Bridge | Zero-G Cyan | `#19b7c9` | `rgb(25, 183, 201)` | `hsl(186, 78%, 44%)` | Connects blue and green in gradients and motion cues. |
| Glow | Plasma Mint | `#43d6a2` | `rgb(67, 214, 162)` | `hsl(159, 64%, 55%)` | Highlights, focus moments, and dark-surface luminosity. |

## Neutrals and surfaces

| Role | Name | HEX | RGB | HSL | Guidance |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Neutral dark | Deep Orbit | `#071a27` | `rgb(7, 26, 39)` | `hsl(204, 70%, 9%)` | Preferred dark background and high-contrast text. |
| Neutral mid | Slate Vector | `#5d6b76` | `rgb(93, 107, 118)` | `hsl(206, 12%, 41%)` | Supporting labels and secondary information. |
| Neutral light | Cloud Field | `#f5faf9` | `rgb(245, 250, 249)` | `hsl(168, 33%, 97%)` | Preferred light background and breathing space. |
| White | Pure White | `#ffffff` | `rgb(255, 255, 255)` | `hsl(0, 0%, 100%)` | Monochrome mark and dark-surface contrast. |

## Usage

Use Lift Blue and Vector Green together as the recognizable AG Manager signature. Zero-G Cyan may bridge them in restrained gradients. Plasma Mint is a highlight, not a replacement primary. Maintain generous clear space around the mark and use the white variant whenever the color mark loses contrast.
